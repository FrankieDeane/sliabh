Sliabh MCP: Argentina's national park trails in Claude

The Sliabh connector lets Claude look up hiking trails in Argentina's 39 national parks and other mountain areas: distance, elevation gain, duration, difficulty, best season, permits, trailheads and safety notes. It uses the same trail data published on sliabh.com.ar.

It is free, read-only and needs no account or sign-in.

Server URL

https://sliabh.com.ar/mcp

Connect

Claude (web, desktop and mobile)

  1. Open Settings → Connectors. If Sliabh is listed in the directory, select it and choose Connect.
  2. Otherwise choose Add custom connector, name it Sliabh, paste https://sliabh.com.ar/mcp and save. There is no authentication step.
  3. In a chat, enable Sliabh from the tools menu and ask about trails.

Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http sliabh https://sliabh.com.ar/mcp

Other MCP clients

Any client that supports the Streamable HTTP transport can connect to the same URL. The server is stateless and answers JSON-RPC over POST.

Example prompts

  • “Which trails can I hike in Nahuel Huapi National Park in under 5 hours?”
  • “Compare Laguna de los Tres and Laguna Torre in El Chaltén: distance, elevation and difficulty.”
  • “I'm staying in Bariloche. Find easy trails within 30 km.”
  • “List the national parks in Patagonia that have mapped trails.”
  • “What do I need to know before hiking to the Lanín base: season, permits and trailhead?”

Tools

All tools are read-only and marked with readOnlyHint: true.

ToolWhat it does
list_national_parksLists the 39 national parks with provinces, region and how many trails Sliabh has mapped in each. Filters: region, province, only parks with trails.
get_national_parkOne park by slug or name (accents optional) with all its mapped trails.
search_trailsSearches trails by keyword, park, province, difficulty, activity, maximum distance, duration or elevation gain. Sort by relevance, distance or elevation. Up to 25 results.
get_trailFull details for one trail: description, trailhead and coordinates, stats, season, permits, safety, access and water notes, and a link to the trail page with the 3D map and GPX download.
find_trails_nearTrails whose trailhead is within a radius of a latitude/longitude, nearest first. Optional difficulty filter.

Every tool takes an optional lang parameter: "es" (default) or "en".

Coverage and limits

  • All 39 national parks are listed. Not every park has mapped trails yet: list_national_parks returns a trail_count for each one, and the catalog grows as trails are added to sliabh.com.ar.
  • Trail stats are reference values. Conditions, closures and permits change: check with the park rangers (guardaparques) and the park's official channels before you go.
  • The connector returns summaries and links, not full GPS tracks. Download the GPX from each trail's page.

Mountain safety: register your trip where the park requires it, carry water and layers, and turn back if the weather changes. Sliabh data doesn't replace local guidance.

Privacy and support

The connector stores no personal data and keeps no record of your questions. Read the privacy policy.

Support: contacto@sliabh.com.ar. You can also report a problem or suggest a trail on GitHub.

En español

El conector de Sliabh le permite a Claude consultar las rutas de trekking de los 39 parques nacionales de Argentina y de otras zonas de montaña: distancia, desnivel, duración, dificultad, mejor época, permisos, punto de partida y avisos de seguridad. Usa los mismos datos que sliabh.com.ar. Es gratis, de solo lectura y no requiere cuenta.

Cómo conectarlo

  1. En Claude, abrí Configuración → Conectores. Si Sliabh aparece en el directorio, elegilo y tocá Conectar.
  2. Si no, elegí Agregar conector personalizado, ponele Sliabh y pegá https://sliabh.com.ar/mcp. No hay paso de autenticación.
  3. En Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http sliabh https://sliabh.com.ar/mcp

Ejemplos

  • “¿Qué rutas puedo hacer en el Parque Nacional Nahuel Huapi en menos de 5 horas?”
  • “Compará Laguna de los Tres y Laguna Torre en El Chaltén.”
  • “Estoy en Bariloche: buscame rutas fáciles a menos de 30 km.”
  • “¿Qué parques nacionales de la Patagonia tienen rutas mapeadas?”

Todos los parques están listados, pero no todos tienen rutas mapeadas todavía. Los datos son de referencia: consultá con los guardaparques antes de salir. Política de privacidad · Soporte: contacto@sliabh.com.ar.
